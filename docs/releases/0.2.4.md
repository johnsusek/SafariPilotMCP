Agents stay out of your way in Safari.

## Changed

- **No window comes forward:** `key` and `form_input` on selects, dates, times and sliders now act through the page script by default, so the pilot window no longer jumps in front of your Safari windows. `compat: true` keeps real input for pages that ignore page events.
- **Smaller screenshots:** full captures are downscaled to fit 1568 px a side and 1.15 megapixels. `region` crops the native capture, so zooms keep full detail.

## Fixed

- **tab_create in the background:** a new pilot tab or window no longer comes in front of the Safari window you are using.
- **Slider overshoot:** `form_input` on sliders that update late, such as YouTube's seek bar, no longer overshoots the target.
- **Trusted Types pages:** `javascript` now works on pages that require Trusted Types, such as YouTube.
